1. Agentic video is here — describe it, get a video

AI agents can now edit, animate, and produce videos from a plain description, NLW argues in today's AI Daily Brief — the same agentic shift that hit text and code is reaching video. The episode walks through what changed and how to use it: meeting highlights cut automatically, concept demos, animated business updates, and customer-education videos that update themselves as the product changes. It lays out three levels for getting started, from editing existing recordings with an agent to building recurring video workflows that run on their own.

Why it matters: Video has been the last major medium to resist the "describe it, get it" pattern — it is labor-intensive, tool-heavy, and hard to iterate. If agents can reliably produce and maintain video the way they now draft and revise text, the cost of keeping visual material current collapses, and every team that communicates in video — sales, support, training, marketing — gets a new workflow to rethink.
