1. NLW takes stock of his own AI stack — and the proactive-agent moment

In the day's only new item across all 17 tracked sources, the AI Daily Brief's Nathaniel Whittemore uses the launch of Claude Duet as an occasion to lay out how he actually uses AI today: strategy and research, coding, content creation, and data analysis, with different models suited to different tasks. He then compares ChatGPT Dots with Claude Duet, and argues that proactive personal agents — tools that act on your behalf rather than waiting to be prompted — may finally be finding their place in his workflow.

Why it matters: The interesting signal here is the framing, not any single product: the frontier is shifting from "ask the chatbot" to agents that proactively do work. When a daily practitioner starts organizing his stack around that shift, it's a sign the agentic era is moving from demo to daily habit.
