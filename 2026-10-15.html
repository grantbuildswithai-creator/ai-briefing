Top stories

1. Modal crosses $1B run rate, raises $750M Series D at $15.75B

Modal, the serverless cloud built for AI workloads, crossed $1B in annualized run rate and announced a $750M Series D at a $15.75B valuation, led by Accel. Usage has tripled since its Series C — more than 15x year-over-year growth. The driver is Sandboxes, the execution environment for LLM-generated code launched in 2023, now a $300M run-rate product on its own: Modal has spun up close to 3 billion sandboxes, scaling to a million concurrent, serving background agents, reinforcement learning, and vibecoding platforms.

Why it matters: NYC's newest AI-infrastructure decacorn. The funding story is really an agent story — agents, not training runs, are driving the fastest-growing infrastructure demand.

Sources: [Latent Space [AINews]](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-modals-1b-arr-16b-series-d)

2. Anthropic publishes its 2026 US midterm election safeguards

Anthropic outlined how it's preparing Claude for this year's US midterms: measuring and preventing political bias, detecting misuse, and routing people to accurate voting information. It used Anthropic Insights to study how people actually use Claude for elections and politics, and says the work builds on the election-safeguards post it published ahead of the 2024 election.

Why it matters: With the midterms weeks away, this is a lab publishing its election playbook — accuracy-routing plus abuse detection rather than refusal-only — a template other labs will be compared against.

Sources: Anthropic News

3. a16z: long-running agents are "winning the relay race" in customer journeys (covered by 2 sources)

a16z published an essay arguing the real payoff for agentic AI is in high-value customer journeys — the kind that used to mean phone menus, hold music, and re-explaining your problem to three agents. Long-running agents that carry context across an entire journey, handing off to humans at the right moments like runners passing a baton, are framed as the next durable wedge for AI in customer operations.

Why it matters: Not a product launch — a framing piece from one of the most-read investor voices, and useful context for where agent startups are expected to find revenue beyond coding.

Sources: a16z.com, a16z News
